The designer

Naveen Kharmawphlang

Creative Director · Founder · Designer

A space is a sculpture. Light is the tool I shape it with.

I begin with what a place already carries — the culture of the people in it, what they have kept, what they want back. A home is a record of the family inside it, and that idea sits at the centre of everything I make.

Home, cafe, restaurant, commercial floor — the question never changes. How someone moves through it. What the budget allows, and where a rule can be bent to make something considered rather than decorated.

The design follows from that, not from a style I arrived with. What I hope you feel, when the work is done, is that the space was always meant to be this way.

— Naveen

01

Interior design consultation

The starting point for every project — a focused conversation, held in person or by video, in which we walk through your space, how you use it, and what you want it to become. You leave with an initial design direction and a clear recommendation for the next step, whether that is a single space, a full home, or a renovation.

  • Pricing: Virtual ₹3,000 · In-person (Shillong) ₹5,500 · Outside Shillong ₹5,500 + travel · With concept note ₹8,500
  • Understanding your space and needs
  • Initial design direction
  • Layout and style discussion
  • Budget alignment conversation
  • Next-step recommendation
02

Space planning and layout design

How a space is organised, and how you move through it. Before any surface or finish is chosen, the plan is resolved — circulation, proportion, where light falls, and how each area earns its place. This is the stage that quietly decides whether a finished space feels effortless or awkward.

  • Revised layout planning
  • Furniture layout
  • Flow between spaces
  • Proportion and scale study
03

3D modelling and visualization

For clients who want to see the space before execution begins. Photoreal views let you make material and layout decisions with confidence, approve a direction before committing to it, and avoid the expensive changes that come from discovering a problem on site.

  • Pricing: Floor plan from ₹5,000 · Single room + 2 views from ₹10,000 · Premium single-room render set from ₹15,000 · Full-home package from ₹45,000 · Extra render ₹4,500/view
  • 3D floor plan
  • Single-space model with two views
  • Premium photoreal render sets
  • Full-home visualization package
  • Additional high-resolution renders
  • 3D animation on request
04

Material and finish selection

Surfaces are chosen for how they wear as much as how they look — timber, stone, plaster, metal, tile and textile, considered together so that the palette holds up across years rather than seasons. Sourcing and sample review are part of the process, not an afterthought.

  • Colour palette
  • Material selection
  • Finish and surface direction
  • Sample review and sourcing
05

Furniture and decor curation

The layer that completes a space. From a single styling consultation to full-home styling, pieces are selected, commissioned or sourced to suit the space they will live in — and arranged with the same attention given to the architecture.

  • Pricing: Styling consultation ₹10,000 · One-space styling from ₹15,000 · Full-home styling from ₹35,000 · Shoot-ready / special occasion on custom quote
  • Styling consultation
  • One-space styling
  • Full-home styling
  • Shoot-ready and special occasion styling
  • Shopping and source guidance
06

Room-wise design packages

For clients who want one space designed properly, without committing to a full project. Each package is self-contained — concept, plan and detail for that space alone — and can be extended into the rest of the home later if you choose.

  • Pricing: Bedroom from ₹25,000 · Living room from ₹35,000 · Kitchen from ₹40,000 · Dining from ₹25,000 · Study/office from ₹20,000 · Bathroom from ₹15,000
  • Bedroom, living, kitchen or dining
  • Study or home office
  • Bathroom
  • Concept and style direction
  • Basic elevation and design details
07

Full home interior design

Concept through to final styling, resolved across an entire home so that each space belongs to the same conversation. Packages are structured by home size, with detailing scaled to how customised the project needs to be.

  • Pricing: Design-only from ₹160–₹200 per sq ft (by size) · 1BHK from ₹1.75 lakh · 2BHK from ₹2.60 lakh · 3BHK from ₹3.80 lakh · 4BHK/Villa on custom quote · Premium bespoke ₹275–₹325 per sq ft
  • 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK packages
  • 4BHK and villa on custom quote
  • Design concept and space planning
  • Ceiling and lighting concept
  • Joinery and carpentry design intent
  • 3D views for key areas
08

Renovation design and supervision

For spaces that need redesign, upgrade, repair, or full transformation. Work begins with an honest assessment of what exists — what can stay, what must change — and ends with a set of drawings and notes your contractor can actually build from.

  • Pricing: Single room from ₹20,000 · Kitchen from ₹35,000 · Bathroom from ₹20,000 · Partial home from ₹80,000 · Full home from ₹175 per sq ft
  • Existing space assessment
  • Revised layout planning
  • Surface and finish update direction
  • Lighting and ceiling recommendations
  • Storage redesign guidance
  • Execution note for contractors
09

Site coordination and design supervision

Design decisions rarely survive contact with a site untouched. This is ongoing involvement through execution — reviewing material as it arrives, coordinating trades, and making the finishing-stage calls that are difficult to resolve from a drawing alone. Available at three levels of intensity depending on how active the project is.

  • Pricing: Basic ₹35,000/month · Moderate ₹55,000/month · Full active ₹75,000/month
  • Basic design supervision
  • Moderate project coordination
  • Full active supervision
  • Custom furniture execution
  • Material review on site
  • Finishing-stage design decisions
10

Turnkey interior design for selected projects

Design and execution managed together, so you have one point of responsibility from first sketch to handover. Charged as a percentage of total project cost and taken on selectively, for private and commercial projects above a minimum value.

  • Pricing: 12% of total project cost · Minimum project value — Private ₹5 lakh, Commercial ₹8 lakh
  • Design development
  • Vendor and contractor coordination
  • Site review
  • Material tracking
  • Design compliance checks
  • Finishing-stage guidance
